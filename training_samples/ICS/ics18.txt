INVENTORY CUSTODIAN SLIP (ICS)
Form used to assign accountability for issued items under government inventory policy.

Sections:
- Quantity, Unit, Amount
- Description and Serial Number (if any)
- Inventory Item Number
- Estimated Useful Life
- Signatures: “Received from” (Supply Officer) and “Received by” (End-user)
