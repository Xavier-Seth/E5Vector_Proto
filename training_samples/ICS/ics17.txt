INVENTORY CUSTODIAN SLIP – DepEd Form (ICS)
Purpose: To record the distribution of tangible items to accountable officers or end-users.
Details include:
• Entity Name and Fund Cluster
• ICS Control Number
• Quantity and Unit of Item
• Description and Serial Number
• Estimated Useful Life
• Unit and Total Cost

Form signed by the AO IV (Supply Unit) and recipient.
Applicable to semi-expendable items below ₱15,000.
