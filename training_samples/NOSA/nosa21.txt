Subject: Implementation of the Fourth Tranche of the Modified Salary Schedule

Pursuant to the provisions of Republic Act No. 11466 (Salary Standardization Law of 2019), the monthly basic salary of civilian personnel shall be adjusted.

Old Rate (Third Tranche): P 33,575
New Rate (Fourth Tranche): P 35,097
Differential: P 1,522

Effective Date: January 1, 2023
