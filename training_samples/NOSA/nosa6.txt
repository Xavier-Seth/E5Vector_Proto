DIVISION MEMORANDUM No. 407, s. 2024
SUBJECT: PRINTING OF NOTICE OF SALARY ADJUSTMENT (NOSA)

In accordance with National Budget Circular No. 594, dated August 12, 2024, under Executive Order (EO) No. 64 s. 2024, the field is directed to print the NOSA by school.

All AO IIs shall facilitate the printing of NOSA in two (2) copies (Division Copy and Personal Copy).
