Computation of Salary Differential (Retroactive)

The Salary Standardization Law (SSL) represents a general increase in the salary schedule.
Since the effectivity is January 1, but the release is March, the employee is entitled to a differential.

New Rate (SG 11): 27,000
Old Rate (SG 11): 25,439
Difference: 1,561 x 3 months = P 4,683.00

Payable immediately upon release of allotment.
