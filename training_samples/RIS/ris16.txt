Requisition and Issue Slip (RIS)
A form used by government offices under COA Appendix 50 to record the request and issuance of materials and supplies.

Typical fields:
- Entity Name, Fund Cluster, and Responsibility Center Code
- Stock Number, Unit, and Description
- Quantity, Purpose, and Remarks
- Signatures: Requested by, Approved by, Issued by, and Received by
