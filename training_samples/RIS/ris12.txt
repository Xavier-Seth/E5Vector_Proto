REQUISITION AND ISSUE SLIP (RIS)
Used to request and issue office or school supplies, materials, or equipment.
Contains the following details:
• Entity Name and Fund Cluster
• Responsibility Center Code
• Quantity and Unit Description
• Purpose of requisition
• Signatures of requester, approver, issuer, and receiver

This form is part of COA Appendix 50 and ensures accountability for issued items.
